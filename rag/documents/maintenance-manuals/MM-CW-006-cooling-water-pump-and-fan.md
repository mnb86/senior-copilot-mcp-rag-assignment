# Cooling Water Pump and Cooling Tower Fan Maintenance Guide

## 1. Cooling Water Pump 301 - Low Discharge Pressure

Low discharge pressure on a cooling water pump usually indicates low basin level at the cooling tower,
a blocked suction screen, air entrainment, or impeller wear. Check the basin level first, then the
suction screen differential. If the pump is running with a low discharge pressure for more than
30 minutes, start the standby pump and inspect the running pump.

## 2. Cooling Water Pump 301 - High Vibration

Check for cavitation (low basin level), coupling misalignment and bearing condition. Vibration above
7.1 mm/s requires a vibration spectrum within 24 hours.

## 3. Cooling Tower Fan 301

High vibration on the cooling tower fan is most often caused by blade imbalance from ice or debris, or by
gearbox wear. Stop the fan if vibration exceeds the trip limit. A Gearbox Oil Low Level alarm requires the
gearbox to be topped up before the next start; do not run the fan with a low gearbox oil level for more
than 4 hours.
