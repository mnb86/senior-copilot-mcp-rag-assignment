1 Introduction

This manual covers condition monitoring and maintenance of the bearings, forced lubrication system and
mechanical seals of boiler feed pumps BFP-101 and BFP-102 (Flowserve HDX 150-8) and their lube oil
console LOS-101.

2 Lubrication System

2.1 Normal operating values
Lube oil header pressure: 1.8 to 2.5 bar(g). Low pressure alarm at 1.2 bar(g).
Lube oil cooler outlet temperature: 40 to 50 degC.
Filter differential pressure: below 1.0 bar with a clean element.

2.2 Low lube oil pressure
A low lube oil pressure condition starves the journal and thrust bearings and results in a bearing
temperature rise within 5 to 15 minutes. Check in order: auxiliary oil pump auto-start, filter
differential pressure, pressure control valve setting, and oil level in the reservoir. Change over to
the standby filter if the differential pressure exceeds 1.0 bar.

2.3 Chattering filter differential pressure alarm
The filter DP alarm frequently chatters around its setpoint during filter loading. This is a known
nuisance alarm; a 10 second on-delay is recommended in the alarm rationalization review. Do not disable
the alarm.

3 Bearings

3.1 High bearing temperature
Bearing temperature alarm is set at 85 degC and trip at 95 degC. On a high bearing temperature alarm:
verify lube oil pressure and cooler outlet temperature, check oil for water contamination, and take an
oil sample for particle count and water content. Increasing lube oil cooler water flow is acceptable as a
temporary measure only when lube oil pressure is normal. Continued operation above 90 degC is not
permitted; transfer load to the standby pump.

3.2 Bearing replacement
Replace bearings when the oil analysis shows babbitt particles or when the temperature exceeds the trip
setting on two occasions within 30 days.

4 Vibration Monitoring

4.1 Limits
Overall velocity alarm 7.1 mm/s, trip 11.0 mm/s, per ISO 10816-7 zone limits for this machine class.

4.2 Diagnosis
Take a vibration spectrum when the alarm is raised. 1x running speed indicates imbalance; 2x indicates
misalignment; broadband high-frequency noise with low suction pressure indicates cavitation. Cavitation
must be corrected at the process side (suction conditions) and not by mechanical maintenance.

5 Mechanical Seals

A seal leak alarm requires a field check within one hour. Minor weeping is acceptable until the next
planned outage; a continuous leak requires changeover to the standby pump and seal replacement.
