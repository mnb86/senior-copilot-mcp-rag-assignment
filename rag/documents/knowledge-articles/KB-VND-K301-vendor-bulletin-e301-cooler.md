# Vendor Service Bulletin - K-301 Discharge Cooler E-301 Fin Fouling

## 1. Observation

Field inspections of air-cooled exchangers of the E-301 type show airborne dust and fibre accumulation on
fin banks after 9 to 12 months of operation. Fouling reduces air flow by up to 30 % and raises process
outlet temperature by 6 to 10 degC in summer conditions, which in turn raises compressor discharge
pressure on the served compressor.

## 2. Recommended Maintenance

Wash the fin banks with low-pressure water (below 10 bar) from the air-outlet side every 6 months, and
inspect fan blade pitch after washing.
